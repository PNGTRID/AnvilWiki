## A true scene first

Day three: your first real visitors. Two kinds of beginners now — one refreshes analytics hourly and spirals; the other never looks and writes by vibes. The right posture sits between: **one fixed weekly time, one reading, three numbers, one decision**. A new site growing from single digits is completely normal — read trends, not absolutes.

## Three panels, one number each

| Panel | The one number you read | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **GSC → Effect** | Which queries show and get clicked | Where Google places me; what players searched to find me |
| **Cloudflare Web Analytics** | Visits + top three pages | How many today; what they love |
| **Clarity** (optional) | Where players get stuck in recordings | Which part of the page slams the door |

## Four pass lines (read against them, not vibes)

| Metric | Pass line / target | If failing |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (click-through rate) | ≥ 2% | Fix title and description — includes "game + query"? Any hook? |
| Daily clicks | 1,000/day is the goal | Single digits are normal early; a flat week is the signal to act (add pages / swap query) |
| Pages per visit | ≥ 1.5 | Below 1.5 = thin internal links: add 1-2 related-article links per page |
| New pages weekly | 10+ | **Add only, don't rewrite old pages** — new pages are the incremental signal to Google |

## Reading discipline: only numbers on panels count

- **Conclusions cite sources**: "traffic grew this week" must point at a number in a panel; un-citable conclusions are inadmissible next week.
- **Actions aren't results**: "published 10 pages", "changed the theme" are things you did, not site outcomes — outcomes are whether CTR, clicks, indexing moved.
- **Overturned conclusions get fixed on the spot**: last week's "this query has no volume" beaten by this week's data? Correct the note now; don't let a stale "current truth" mislead next week's you.

## Three classic mistakes (made for you in advance)

- **Refreshing the panel daily**: data barely moves weekly; hourly reads harvest only anxiety. Fix the weekly slot.
- **Chasing vanity numbers**: total visits feel good, but "sources + click queries" drive action — read to decide, not to feel good.
- **Redesigning on every wiggle**: algorithms tweak monthly; days of movement are noise — act after a flat week, not a bad day.

## Three words to know (just these)

- **Impression**: your page appeared in results once — clicked or not.
- **CTR**: impressions converted to clicks — the report card of your title and description.
- **Trend**: today's points joined into a line — the only shape worth watching on a new site.

## ✅ Acceptance (all must hold)

- ☐ Your weekly reading slot is on the calendar
- ☐ You can quote one number from each panel
- ☐ This week produced one number→action decision (even if the action is "keep watching")

## A true scene first

Ads approved, balance accruing — and here's the classic crash: the site owner assumes payouts are automatic, hits $100, and discovers the PIN was never verified. Payment suspended; a free month wasted. This lesson lays out the three verifications with waiting periods so you can **do all of them on approval day** and let the cycles run in parallel with the earning.

### Verification one: tax info (the W-8BEN form, 10 minutes)

1. AdSense → **Payments** → **Manage settings** → **Tax info** → submit W-8BEN
2. Fill your identity truthfully; country: China; **claim the tax treaty benefit** (Royalties category) — the China-US treaty rate is **10%**; without it the default withholding is **30%**: the same revenue, twenty points apart

**You'll see**: tax info status flips to approved.

### Verification two: the address PIN postcard (2-4 weeks of waiting, the stickiest one)

- At a **$10** balance, Google auto-generates a 6-digit PIN and mails it to your account address (to China usually from Singapore/Malaysia, 2-4 weeks, with real loss rates)
- Received: **Payments** → **Verify** → enter the PIN — instant pass
- **Unverified for four months after generation, Google pauses ad serving on your site** — the money isn't lost, but income stops until you verify. Check your address now, not at $10

You get **four mailing attempts** (one automatic + three manual resends); if all fail, use the official form for identity verification (ID + address proof).

### Verification three: identity check + wire transfer (10 minutes each)

- Some accounts trigger KYC identity verification — upload documents as prompted. Counter-intuitive detail: **the identity check uses your name in Chinese characters** (matching your ID), while the bank payee uses **pinyin** — the two looking different is correct; don't "fix" it
- **Payments** → Add payment method → **Wire transfer**: payee name in **pinyin** (matching the bank account), account number, bank name, and the **SWIFT code** (bank website, or ask for "the SWIFT code for receiving USD wires"); a normal domestic debit card works. Set it to automatic payout

### The payout rhythm and currency exchange

Auto-payout at **$100**, dispatched **monthly on the 21st**, arriving in 1-3 business days; convert USD to local currency in your banking app once it lands. Keep the [official payout steps](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709858) bookmarked.

## Three classic mistakes (made for you in advance)

- **Skipping the treaty benefit on W-8BEN**: silently donating 20 points of tax — over a year, real money.
- **An unrealistic mailing address**: the PIN never arrives, verification stalls, ads pause — use an address that can actually receive mail.
- **Researching payouts at $100**: every verification has a waiting period; do them on day one so the waiting overlaps with the earning.

## Three words to know (just these)

- **W-8BEN**: the tax declaration a non-US individual files with a US company — one form sets your withholding rate.
- **SWIFT code**: your bank's international wire address; required for USD, ask your bank.
- **Payout**: the monthly dispatch of balances over $100, executed on the 21st.

## ✅ Acceptance (all must hold)

- ☐ Tax info approved at the 10% rate
- ☐ Address double-checked; PIN in transit (or verified)
- ☐ Wire method added, automatic payout on

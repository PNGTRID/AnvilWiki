## Your 30-day subway map

From zero to an earning site is six stops. See the whole route before boarding:

| Stop | Lessons | Time | You carry away |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① Pick & validate | 03-08 | 2 days | A decided game + the day-one 10-page list |
| ② Stand it up | 09-12 | 1 day | A working site that is yours |
| ③ Content engine | 13-17 | Week one | First 10-15 pages passing QA |
| ④ Launch & index | 18-21 | 3 days | Live worldwide + Google indexing |
| ⑤ Monetize | 22-24 | After qualifying | Ad slots live + payout channel ready |
| ⑥ Operate | 25-29 | 30 min weekly | A stable update-and-rank rhythm |

**Never skip a stop.** Each stop's output is the next stop's raw material: without ①'s query list, ③'s pages target things nobody searches; without ③'s 15 pages, ⑤'s ad application fails review. Every day a skip saves you, you repay double later.

## How to brief AI (three rules, used all through this manual)

Every hands-on step in this manual ships a copy-paste AI prompt. Before using them, memorize three rules:

1. **Feed material; never let it guess.** Every `<placeholder>` in a prompt gets replaced with real material: real Trends data, real results-page listings. AI-invented game facts (codes, stats) destroy site trust — with material supplied, there is nothing to invent.
2. **Ask for tables, not prose.** Tell AI to "output a table: priority / page / target query / material needed" — tables paste into your worksheet; prose does not.
3. **Make it ask you back.** End prompts with "list what's missing and ask me" — AI then puts the gaps on the table instead of silently filling them with fake data.

Uniform usage: copy the whole block → replace every `<placeholder>` → send. **Planning prompts** let AI advise but never touch files; **page-production prompts** are the ones that write into your repo.

## Three classic mistakes (made for you in advance)

- **Skipping stops**: jumping straight to "turn on ads", then discovering the application needs 15-20 pages. Every stop's gate is written on the road.
- **Reading without doing**: every lesson ends with an acceptance checklist — no output, no moving on. This is a manual, not a novel.
- **Grinding when stuck**: every lesson carries "three classic mistakes"; still stuck, throw your screenshot and question straight at AI — it is your teaching assistant on call.

## A true scene first

On day four of content production the urge arrives: have AI generate 50 pages and fill the site. The hard-won lesson — **too many pages in version one dilutes quality and drags the whole site's rankings**. Google grades a site holistically; 20 weak pages inside 50 drag the 30 good ones down with them.

## The three-line schedule

1. **A first release of 10-15 core pages is enough** — codes, tier list, guide queries first. That's precisely the output of the last four lessons: 1 codes page + 1 beginner guide + 3 boss guides + 1 tier list + 1 FAQ page + 3 window-query pages (Lesson 8's list, cashed in).
2. **Add 10+ pages weekly** — `draft: true` drafts verified and flipped one by one; new query pages keep following the Lesson 8 list downward.
3. **Batch production obeys the same rule** — when you later mass-produce dozens of pages (Lesson 23's pipeline), the discipline stands: **generate in batches, ship in batches**.

## When the batch is green, walk away

First release all green? Move to the next stop (launch) — **don't polish forever**. The practical reason: unpublished content lives in a vacuum; you have no idea how players and Google respond. Post-launch data (which queries got impressions, which pages got clicks) is the best navigation for the next batch. Perfectionism is procrastination with better handwriting.

## Three classic mistakes (made for you in advance)

- **Dumping every draft at once**: dilutes quality, drags rankings — batching is a discipline, not a suggestion.
- **Hitting a page count at quality's expense**: can't fill 15 good ones? Ship 10. The Lesson 16 quality line doesn't bend.
- **Flipping drafts without verifying**: bulk-flipping drafts is bulk bare-shipping. Flipping follows verification; only generation comes with a button.

## Three words to know (just these)

- **First release**: the opening batch of core pages, 10-15 — the site's storefront and starting line.
- **In waves**: a fixed rhythm of 10+ weekly — slower is fine, messy never.
- **Flip live**: verification-then-publish, one draft at a time — never one button.

## ✅ Acceptance (all must hold)

- ☐ The first release of 10-15 pages all passed the Lesson 16 pipeline
- ☐ Remaining drafts all carry draft flags, with a weekly flip hour on your calendar
- ☐ You decided to stop and move to launch — this line matters most

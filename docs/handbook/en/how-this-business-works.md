## Walk the real chain first

You search Google for a game's redeem codes, click a site near the top, and the page shows some ad slots. In those few seconds, money moves:

```
Player searches → Google ranks your page → player clicks in → ads get seen → advertiser pays → you get a share
```

That is the entire business. You build the bridge between link 1 (content) and link 4 (ads); Google delivers the visitors, advertisers pay for the attention you aggregate. **You never sell anything to players** — guides are free to read, and advertisers fund the transaction.

## Where the money enters, and the honest order of magnitude

Ad money settles per thousand impressions/clicks. For game guide sites the common ballpark is: **every 1,000 page visits brings roughly a few dimes to a few dollars** (it swings hard with player geography, season, and ad performance — this number builds intuition only; it is not a promise).

From that magnitude, three iron laws of the business:

1. **One page is not enough — you need dozens.** A single page getting a few dozen visits a day earns nothing; dozens of pages each targeting one query are the real traffic base.
2. **Return visits beat one-time visits.** Players come back daily for a codes page, so the same traffic earns more reliably. That is why a later lesson puts the codes page first in the writing order.
3. **Month one is all cost, no income.** Content takes time to accumulate, Google takes time to index, and ads unlock only after you qualify. The normal state of week one: site alive, wallet untouched.

## Three classic mistakes (made for you in advance)

- **"Build it and they will come."** Building the site just opens the shop; until Google knows you, nobody walks past. Getting people here is link 1 — content aimed at real queries.
- **"I need to learn programming first."** This template squeezes the tech down to "follow a few commands" — you will not write a line of code in this manual. The real homework is picking the right game and producing content at volume.
- **"Day one is ads day."** Ads are a switch that renders nothing while its value is empty — ten minutes to turn on. Turned on with no content and no traffic, revenue is zero. Build the engine first.

## Three words to know (just these)

- **Traffic**: how many people visit your site. The raw material of the business.
- **Monetization**: turning traffic into money — in this manual, specifically ads.
- **SEO**: the craft of getting Google to rank your pages — that is, making link 1 work. The whole manual teaches it.

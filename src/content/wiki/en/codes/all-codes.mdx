## How do I redeem codes in Anvil Quest?

Redeeming takes about twenty seconds once you know where the box hides:

1. Press the **gear icon** (Settings) in the top-right corner of the screen — it is there from the tutorial forge onward, so you can redeem NEWPLAYER within your first minute of play.
2. Open the **Account** tab and click **Redeem Code**.
3. Paste the code exactly as written in the list above and press **Confirm**. Codes are case-sensitive and the dash in FORGE-2026 is a real hyphen, not an em dash — type it if paste mangles it.
4. Rewards land in your inventory immediately. Gold and charms appear in the currency bar; XP buffs start their timer **the moment you redeem**, so don't redeem EMBERBORN while you're about to log off.

**Redeem troubleshooting** — if a code from the active list is rejected:

- **"Invalid code"**: it most likely expired since our last daily test pass. Tell us in the comments section of this page and we'll re-test within the day.
- **"Already redeemed"**: reward codes are one-per-account. This includes NEWPLAYER, whose starter bundle is tied to account creation date, not level.
- **Typo from hand-typing**: copy-paste. The most common failure is a lowercase L read as a capital I in ASHENKEEP.

## Where do new codes come from?

The developer releases codes in three predictable patterns, which is why this page can promise daily coverage:

1. **Update milestones** — every major patch (v2.4, v2.5…) ships a celebration code that lives about two weeks. FORGE-2026 came with the v2.5 forge update.
2. **Calendar events** — the anniversary ANVIL-DAWN code runs a full month (September 15 expiry) and historically returns each year with a new reward tier.
3. **Creator partnerships** — the rarest type: a code embedded in a sponsored stream. These usually have a 500-redemption cap, so they burn off in hours; when one drops, this page is the fastest place to catch it.

The official Discord `#announcements` channel is the source the developer uses first — everything there gets tested and added here within a day.

## Why do codes stop working?

Codes expire on a schedule set by the developer (usually monthly or tied to update milestones), and some are one-per-account. Expiry is enforced server-side: when a code's date passes, the redemption endpoint rejects it even if the UI still offers the input box.

If a code above fails, it likely expired since our last test — check back tomorrow. Expired codes stay listed on purpose: FROSTPIKE's entry is how you can tell at a glance that this page actually prunes dead codes instead of farming a list from months ago. Re-release attempts are real: BETA-HERO briefly worked again during the June anniversary, which is why "expired" codes are worth a retry exactly once per patch.

## What to spend code gold on

Gold from codes compounds fastest when it buys permanent power rather than consumables:

1. **First priority: the Frostpike recipe** (300 gold from Mara's anvil, or free if you didn't skip the tutorial crafting). It's an S-tier weapon per our [weapon tier list](/guides/weapon-tier-list/) and doubles your damage against every fire-weak boss.
2. **Second: armor crafting materials.** The [Emberforged armor set](/items/emberforged-armor-set/) costs more in [forging materials](/items/forging-materials-guide/) than in gold, but its crafting fees add up to ~800 gold — code gold covers it.
3. **Last: potions** for boss attempts. One Greater Frost Potion (60 gold) turns the [Emberfang](/bosses/emberfang/) enrage phase from a coinflip into a formality.

Coins from codes go furthest early: the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) lists what to buy first, and the [Emberfang guide](/bosses/emberfang/) is the first boss those upgrades unlock.

## How to Make Sure You Actually Get the Rewards

This is the step people skip and then regret. Pre-registration rewards are delivered to accounts, and the delivery breaks when your registration identity and your play identity do not match. Follow this checklist:

1. **Use your real, permanent account.** If you pre-order on Google Play with a throwaway Google account and later play on your main, the reward may be locked to the wrong identity.
2. **Keep the same email.** The official site signup and your game account should share one email address you control.
3. **Do not delete the store page reservation** before launch — cancelled pre-orders can remove you from the eligibility list.
4. **On launch day, check your in-game mailbox first.** Based on how comparable campaigns run, milestone rewards will most likely arrive via in-game mail on the account you registered with — no code entry needed.
5. **Claim mail promptly.** In-game mail in similar titles expires after roughly 30 days.

If you pre-registered months ago and have since changed email addresses or plan to switch platforms (say, Android to Steam), sort that out **before** September 16. Post-launch support tickets about undelivered pre-registration rewards are, in every F2P game's history, a slow and painful queue.

## FAQ

### What do I get for pre-registering for Aniimo?

At minimum, the unlocked 5 million milestone launch reward pack. If the community reaches higher tiers before launch, everyone who pre-registered also receives Glimmer ×200 (10M), Aniipod Ultra ×2 (15M), an exclusive avatar frame (20M), and the Sunlit Meadow limited outfit (30M).

### How do I claim Aniimo pre-registration rewards?

You do not need to do anything beyond pre-registering with the correct account. Rewards are expected to be delivered via in-game mail after the September 16, 2026 launch to the account that pre-registered. Log in on launch day and check your mailbox.

### Can I still pre-register for Aniimo?

Yes. As of August 15, 2026, the campaign is open on the official website, the Steam wishlist, and the App Store and Google Play pages. Any pre-registration completed before the September 16 launch qualifies for all unlocked milestones.

### Do pre-registration rewards work across platforms?

The milestone counter is global, and Aniimo is cross-platform, so rewards should apply to the account you registered with regardless of where you play. The safest approach is registering through the official site with the same email you will use to play.

### Is the Sunlit Meadow outfit free?

Yes — if the 30 million pre-registration milestone is reached before launch, the Sunlit Meadow outfit is granted free to every pre-registered player. It is a campaign-exclusive item and is not sold separately.

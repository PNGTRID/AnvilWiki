## What is CloudNote?

CloudNote is a structured notes app: notebooks and tags, bidirectional links between notes, web clipping, and full-text search that actually finds things. The design bet is that notes outlive apps — export is open-format and history is versioned. Four surfaces matter in practice:

1. **Notes and links** — markdown-friendly editing with backlinks, so research notes connect instead of piling up.
2. **Sync** — instant across devices; Basic limits you to two, which is the main reason people outgrow the free tier.
3. **Clipping** — a browser extension that saves articles into your notebook with the source URL preserved.
4. **Shared workspaces** — on Business, team notebooks with roles and admin export.

## Who CloudNote is for — and when to pick something else

**A good fit when:** your notes are reference material you will search for years — research, meeting archives, a personal wiki. The versioned history and open export make it a safe long-term home.

**Look elsewhere when:** you mainly sketch and present rather than write — the design-tool subscription on this site fits that job better (see the [PixelPad design tool](/items/pixelpad-design-tool/) card, with its own [coupon page](/codes/pixelpad-codes/)). And if your note-taking lives inside documents, a writing-suite subscription may beat a standalone notes app entirely.

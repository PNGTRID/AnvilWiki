Pyra is the first real DPS check in Anvil Quest. She has a strict 4-minute enrage timer, and if you don't burn through her 18,000 HP fast enough, the arena fills with unavoidable fire and the run ends.

## Boss Overview

| Stat              | Value                                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Recommended Level | 22+                                     |
| HP                | 18,000                                  |
| Enrage Timer      | 4:00                                    |
| Weakness          | Ice                                     |
| Resistance        | Fire                                    |
| Location          | Emberfall Crater, Shattered Isles Act 2 |

You need an average of 75 DPS to clear the enrage. Most undergeared players sit around 50–60 DPS, which is why this fight gates progression into Act 3.

## The DPS Check

Pyra is the only boss in the game with a hard enrage timer — every other boss can be outlasted. This means defensive builds that work elsewhere fail here. You must optimize for damage.

**Minimum DPS benchmarks** (solo, no buffs):

- Warrior (Crit build): 70 DPS — just barely passes
- Mage (Ice spec): 90 DPS — comfortable
- Rogue (Backstab): 110 DPS — fastest clears
- Cleric: 40 DPS — will fail enrage solo, requires a duo

## Mechanics

### Meteor Shower

Every 30 seconds, Pyra marks three spots on the ground. After 2 seconds, meteors land on those spots, leaving fire puddles that persist for 20 seconds.

**Counter**: The arena is large enough for 6 puddles before space gets tight. Position so meteors land near the edges, not the center, to preserve your safe zone.

### Flame Whip (frontal cone)

Pyra winds up for 1.2 seconds, then attacks in a 180° cone in front of her. Deals 600 damage and applies a burn that ticks for 80/second for 5 seconds.

**Counter**: She always faces her current aggro target. If you're melee, stand at her side or back — never front. Ranged players should never get hit by this.

### Enrage (4:00)

At the 4-minute mark, Pyra casts Inferno: the entire arena ignites, ticking 200 damage/second to everyone. This is not survivable.

**Counter**: There is none. Kill her before 4:00.

## Recommended Loadouts

### Mage (Ice Spec) — recommended

- Weapon: Frostpike Staff
- Armor: Glacial Robe (+30 ice damage)
- Skills: Ice Lance (spam), Frost Nova (break aggro)
- Potion: Greater Intellect Elixir

This is the most consistent Pyra kill. Ice Lance out-ranges her Flame Whip, and Frost Nova dumps aggro if she turns on you.

### Rogue (Backstab)

- Weapon: Shadowfang Daggers
- Armor: Crit set (aim for 60% crit chance)
- Skills: Backstab, Shadowstep, Poison Vial
- Potion: Haste Potion

Rogue clears Pyra fastest (~2:30) but requires practiced positioning. Shadowstep behind her during every Flame Whip wind-up for free backstabs.

### Warrior (Crit)

- Weapon: Stormcaller Greatsword
- Armor: Crimson Plate (fire resist)
- Skills: Whirlwind, Battle Cry, Shield Wall
- Potion: Greater Strength

The safest loadout if you can hit the DPS threshold. Shield Wall lets you tank one Flame Whip per minute as a panic button.

## Common Mistakes

- **Bringing a fire weapon.** Emberclaw is great for Gelum but Pyra is fire-resistant. Bring Frostpike or Stormcaller instead.
- **Ignoring meteor positioning.** Letting puddles spawn in the center shrinks your safe zone and forces you into her Whip cone.
- **Popping potions too early.** Wait until Phase 2 (50% HP) to use your elixir — the buff lasts 60 seconds and you want it overlapping enrage push.

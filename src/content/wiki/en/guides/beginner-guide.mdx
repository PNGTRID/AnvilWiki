Welcome to Anvil Quest. This guide walks you through your first two hours — from character creation to defeating your first world boss. Follow this in order and you'll hit level 10 with a solid foundation.

## Step 1: Pick Your Starting Class

You choose one of four classes at character creation. You cannot change this later without paying 500 gems, so pick carefully.

| Class   | Difficulty | Playstyle             | Recommended For                 |
| ------- | ---------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Warrior | Easy       | High HP, simple melee | First-time players              |
| Mage    | Medium     | Ranged burst, fragile | Players who like positioning    |
| Rogue   | Hard       | High crit, melee      | Experienced action game players |
| Cleric  | Medium     | Support/healing       | Players who duo with friends    |

**Beginner recommendation: Warrior.** The extra HP forgives mistakes while you learn enemy patterns. Switch to Rogue or Mage once you understand the game.

## Step 2: Complete the Tutorial Forge (0:00 – 0:20)

The tutorial is unskippable but only takes 20 minutes. It teaches you:

- Basic movement (WASD + space to jump, shift to dash)
- How to swing your weapon (left click)
- How to craft your first item at the anvil
- The dialogue system (press E to interact)

You'll end the tutorial at level 3 with a Rusty Iron Sword and 100 coins.

**Do not skip any crafting interactions.** The tutorial forge gives you the Iron Dagger recipe for free. If you skip it, I have to buy the recipe later for 300 coins.

## Step 3: Reach Level 5 in Oakwood Vale (0:20 – 0:50)

After the tutorial, the game drops you in Oakwood Vale. Clear these quests in order:

1. **"Speak to Blacksmith Mara"** — walk 30 seconds north. Free 50 coins.
2. **"Gather 10 Copper Ore"** — the mine is east of Mara's shop. Ore respawns every 45 seconds, so this takes ~5 minutes.
3. **"Craft the Copper Sword"** — back at Mara's anvil. This replaces your Rusty Iron Sword and triples your damage.
4. **"Kill 5 Forest Boars"** — each boar gives 80 XP. You'll hit level 5 mid-way through.

At level 5, unlock your first skill. Warriors should take **Battle Cry** (+20% damage for 10 seconds). Mages take **Firebolt**. Rogues take **Backstab**. Clerics take **Heal**.

## Step 4: First Dungeon — Frostspire Approach (0:50 – 1:20)

At level 5, the path north opens to Frostspire Keep. On the way, you'll pass through the Frostspire Approach, a short dungeon with ice-elemental enemies.

**Key tips:**

- Bring 5 Health Potions (buy from Mara for 25 coins each). Ice enemies hit hard.
- The Frost Wisp miniboss at the end drops a Glacial Gem — save this for later crafting.
- Reach level 8 before fighting Gelum at the end of the dungeon. He's a real boss, not a trash mob.

## Step 5: Defeat Gelum (1:20 – 1:50)

Gelum is your first world boss. At level 8 with a Copper Sword (or better, a Frostpike if you crafted one), he's beatable. Read our full [Gelum Boss Guide](/bosses/gelum) for the phase-by-phase breakdown.

The short version: strafe right to dodge his ice shards, jump his frozen ground rings, and save your dash for his Phase 3 enrage.

## Step 6: Unlock the Shattered Isles (1:50 – 2:00)

Defeating Gelum opens the portal to the Shattered Isles — the game's main progression zone. From here, the real game begins:

- New quest hubs every 3 levels
- World bosses every 5 levels
- Crafting tiers unlock at levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50
- Codes start giving meaningful rewards (check our [codes page](/codes) daily)

## Common Newbie Mistakes

- **Spending gems on cosmetics early.** Save gems for the class-change item or inventory expansions. Cosmetics can wait.
- **Selling the Glacial Gem.** You need 5 of these to craft your first Frostpike weapon. They're soulbound, so you can't trade for them.
- **Skipping side quests.** Oakwood Vale side quests give a total of 400 coins and 2 Health Potions. Worth the 15 minutes.
- **Fighting Gelum under level 8.** Possible but miserable. Go grind to 8 first.

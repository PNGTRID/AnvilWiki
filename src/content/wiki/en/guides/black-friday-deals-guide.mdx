Black Friday is the one week a year when software vendors compete to give money back, and it rewards preparation more than speed. The deepest cuts of 2026 are already listed on our codes pages — a 50% cut on a design tool and a 40% yearly discount on a notes app — but catching them is a process, not a lucky scroll. This guide covers the 2026 calendar, how to build a shortlist worth buying from, and the verification habit that keeps fake deals out of your cart.

## When do Black Friday SaaS deals start in 2026?

Black Friday itself is Friday, November 27, 2026, with Cyber Monday following on November 30. The SaaS industry, however, never confines itself to one weekend:

1. **Early window (Nov 13-20)** — productivity and developer tools open first, usually with "early access" pricing that matches their Black Friday rate.
2. **Core weekend (Nov 25-30)** — every vendor's flagship deal runs, and the deepest single cuts appear here.
3. **Extended tail (Dec 1-7)** — a second wave of vendors extends "Black Friday pricing" through the first week of December; latecomers catch most of the same codes.

The practical takeaway: shortlist in the first week of November, buy in the core weekend, and treat the tail as a safety net rather than a plan.

## How to build a deal shortlist before the sales open

Deals bought in a hurry are the ones regretted in January. Spend one evening before the window opens on this checklist:

1. **List the tools you already pay for.** Renewal dates matter — a deal that lands just before your renewal is worth double, because the discount locks in at renewal instead of fighting your current cycle.
2. **List the tools you were already going to buy.** A 50% discount on something you do not need is still 50% wasted. This season's shortlist for us is a design tool and a notes app — both covered on this site.
3. **Note each tool's normal price.** A "70% off" banner on an inflated list price is a classic move; the number that matters is the final yearly total versus the vendor's standard rate.
4. **Find the official source for each deal now**, before the inbox fills with affiliate noise. Bookmark the vendor's own deals page — you will use it for verification below.

## How to verify a deal before you pay

The 2026 search results are full of recycled 2025 codes and affiliate pages that never tested anything. Three checks filter almost all of it out:

1. **Match the code to an official announcement.** A coupon that appears only on aggregator sites, with no vendor deals page or newsletter behind it, does not exist. Our stacking guide shows the same discipline applied to discount layers.
2. **Check the expiry is real.** Vendors enforce expiry server-side — a "still working!" claim from a stale blog post means nothing. The codes pages on this site re-test on a schedule and show the last verification date on every page.
3. **Watch the billing cycle.** The deepest Black Friday numbers are almost always yearly-only. Monthly billing with a smaller coupon can still win if you only need the tool for a quarter — the math is in our [SaaS discount stacking guide](/guides/saas-discount-stacking-guide/).

If a deal fails all three checks, close the tab. No discount is worth a card detail on a lookalike checkout domain.

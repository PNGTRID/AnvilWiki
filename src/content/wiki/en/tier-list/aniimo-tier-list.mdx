## What This Aniimo Tier List Covers

Aniimo releases September 16, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, iOS and Android, and the final closed beta gave the community its clearest look yet at how the creature roster performs in real combat across Idyll. With roughly 90 creatures visible in the Aniilog during the beta, it is already possible to separate the genuine meta staples from the filler.

This Aniimo tier list ranks every notable creature we tested, using each Aniimo's maximum-stage Total Attributes as the backbone and then adjusting for role value, dual-element coverage and ATK ceilings. Rankings are **based on the final closed beta; we will update after launch** if Pawprint Studio tunes stats on day one.

A quick refresher: every Aniimo grows through **Lumin, Gamma and Nova** stages (this list compares Nova final forms); the roles are **DPS, Heal, Support, BREAK and REGEN** — BREAK units shatter boss guard bars, REGEN units provide sustained recovery; and **Twine** lets you transform into any Aniimo you own, so a high-tier pick doubles as a traversal and puzzle tool.

## How We Ranked the Best Aniimos

Four criteria decided every placement:

1. **Total Attributes at Nova.** The strongest signal from beta data — the Nova roster clusters between 505 and 590, and those gaps matter in endgame content.
2. **Role scarcity.** BREAK and REGEN slots are rarer than DPS slots, so an equal-stat BREAK unit grades higher than yet another attacker.
3. **Dual elements.** Inferlupa (Fire/Dark) and Witchin (Grass/Dark) cover two matchup lanes with one party slot — premium value in a 10-element game.
4. **ATK ceilings for damage dealers.** Stellarys sits at only 505 Total Attributes, but its 145 ATK is the highest raw attack in the beta.

## How to Use This List at Launch

One warning: day-one balance patches are likely, and Egg Heist can shift the meta toward speed over bulk. Treat S tier as investment targets, not guarantees — resonance, training and Twine skill matter as much as raw stats. This list will be re-tested against launch numbers once servers open.

## FAQ

### Is this Aniimo tier list final?

No. It is based on the final closed beta; we will update after launch with day-one stats and any balance changes.

### What is the single best Aniimo in the beta?

Shrubclaw, at 590 Total Attributes with 115 physical defense, was the strongest overall creature in the final beta.

### Who hits the hardest?

Stellarys owns the highest ATK stat in the beta at 145, followed by Thornblade at 141 and Ignitis at 140.

### Do starter Aniimos stay viable?

Yes, through their lines. Skippy becomes the 520-total healer Glacy, and Emberpup becomes the 515-total Scorchhowl — mid-pack at Nova, not meta.

### Does tier matter for Twine and exploration?

Far less. Twine value comes from movement and puzzle abilities, so a C-tier Aniimo can still be your most-used exploration form.

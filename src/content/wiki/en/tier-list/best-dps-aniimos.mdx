## The Best DPS Aniimos in the Final Beta

Damage dealers are the most crowded role in Aniimo — more than a third of the beta's Nova-stage roster is some flavor of DPS. That abundance makes picking worth your resources genuinely hard, so this ranking leans on the two numbers that separated winners from filler in our testing: **ATK** (raw damage ceiling) and **Total Attributes** (survivability and scaling headroom).

All stats below are **based on the final closed beta; we will update after launch** if day-one tuning shifts the rankings. One spoiler up front: the best Aniimo attacker is not the creature with the best Total Attributes.

## How to Actually Use Top DPS in Aniimo

Three lessons from beta endgame testing:

1. **BREAK first, burst second.** Guard bars are real, and a Stellarys that dumps its kit into an unbroken boss wastes half its damage. Pair your carry with a BREAK anchor — Shrubclaw (590), Tuckin (581), Tubster or Helgon (580) — then unload during the break window.
2. **Match the lane, not just the number.** A 125-ATK Sherro into a fire-weak boss beats a 145-ATK Stellarys into a dark-resistant one. Check the element chart before committing resources.
3. **Dual coverage is DPS insurance.** Witchin and Inferlupa are not DPS units, but their dual typings (Grass/Dark, Fire/Dark) let your one dedicated attacker always fight into a favorable lane.

A final note on pace of investment: Nova-stage DPS unlocks are tied to training and resonance, and attack-boosting materials were the tightest resource in the beta economy. Pick one carry from the table above, commit everything to it, and leave the 125-ATK tier alone until your squad's backline is complete — a slightly weaker attacker with a healer behind it clears more content than a stronger one without.

## FAQ

### Who is the best DPS Aniimo?

Stellarys, with 145 ATK — the highest attack stat recorded in the final beta. Its 505 Total Attributes are low for a Nova, so it plays as a glass cannon.

### Is ATK or Total Attributes more important for DPS?

ATK decides your damage ceiling; Total Attributes decides whether you live long enough to use it. The 135-ATK/515-total band (Fenmane, Blazen, Irisal) is the sweet spot for most players.

### Are starter lines viable DPS?

Mid-tier. Scorchhowl (515) is serviceable, and Celestis → Stellarys turns an early catch into the best attacker in the game.

### What about Malevsera?

Its 525 total leads all offensive Aniimos, but its role label was incomplete in beta data — treat it as a strong bet, not a confirmed pick, until launch.

### Do I need more than one DPS?

Two is usually right: one lane-matched carry plus a flexible second. Five attackers is a trap — one healer and one BREAK unit convert damage into clears.

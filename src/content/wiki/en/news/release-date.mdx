## Aniimo Officially Launches September 16, 2026

The news every creature hunter has been waiting for finally landed on August 13: according to the official announcement, Aniimo will release on **September 16, 2026** — and it will hit every single platform on the same day. The reveal was quickly picked up by Gematsu, FinalWeapon, Insider Gaming and other outlets, confirming what fans had been hoping for since the game's surprise debut at the June 2025 Xbox Games Showcase.

That makes the wait surprisingly short: just over a month. The timing lines up neatly with the game's testing schedule — Aniimo wrapped up its second closed beta this past July on PC, iOS and Android, and developer Pawprint Studio has clearly been racing toward gold master ever since. For a game that only announced itself fourteen months ago, a simultaneous worldwide, all-platform launch is an ambitious statement of confidence from the studio and publisher Kingsglory, the FunPlus subsidiary handling publishing duties.

## All Platforms at Launch — No Staggered Rollout

Whether you play on a PS5, an Xbox Series X|S, a gaming rig, or a phone, you start catching creatures on the continent of Idyll the same day as everyone else.

## No Early Access — Everyone Starts Together

In an era where premium editions routinely buy days of head start, Pawprint Studio is doing the opposite. According to the official announcement, there is **no early access period** of any kind. Advanced Edition and Collector's Edition buyers, Game Pass subscribers, and completely free players all set foot in Idyll on September 16.

For a creature-collecting game specifically, this is a bigger deal than it sounds. The genre lives on community discovery — shared tier debates, coordinated egg hunts, and the collective shock of a Sparkling Form appearing in someone's stream. A synchronized start keeps the meta conversational instead of fragmenting it into haves and have-nots in week one, and nobody can grind an unbeatable Egg Heist squad before the servers fill up with real competition.

## FAQ

### When does Aniimo release?

Aniimo releases on **September 16, 2026**, according to the official announcement made on August 13, 2026 and reported by Gematsu, FinalWeapon, and Insider Gaming.

### Which platforms is Aniimo launching on?

PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store), iOS, and Android — all on the same September 16 release date with no staggered rollout.

### Is there early access for Aniimo?

No. According to the official announcement, there is no early access period. Free players, paid edition buyers, and Game Pass subscribers all start on September 16, 2026.

### Is Aniimo free to play?

Yes, the base game is free-to-play on every platform. Optional paid editions (Advanced Edition at $9.99 and Collector's Edition at $19.99) add cosmetics, a battle pass, and bonus items, but entry costs nothing.

### Can I pre-register for Aniimo?

Yes. Pre-registration is open on the official website and mobile stores, and the game has already passed 5 million global pre-registrations, with additional ladder rewards unlocking at higher milestones.
